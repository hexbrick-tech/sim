# SIM Concept Index / Glossary

This document is a navigation and terminology index for the SIM canon.

It does not replace the definitions and boundaries established in the canonical volumes. Its purpose is to make terminology, translation choices, contrasts, and canonical locations easier to observe.

The English canon remains authoritative. Japanese terms listed here are the official translation mappings used by this repository.

## Core Concepts

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meta-Observationalism | メタ観測主義論 | A philosophical position in which observation, the observer, and relevant observation conditions may themselves remain observable. | Not relativism; not an attempt to create a perspective-free observer. | [Vol. 1](./docs/en/vol1-meta-observationalism.md) |
| Reflexive Observation | 再帰観測 | Observation that can turn back toward the observer, perspective, conditions, or acts through which the observation was produced. | **Reflexive** concerns observing the observation/observer itself. | Semantic Authority | 意味の権威 | The person, source, process, or context entitled to define meaning for the matter being considered. | Evidence may support an observation without having semantic authority. | [Vol. 3 §14](./docs/en/vol3-basics-of-sim.md#14-semantic-authority) |
| Semantic Leakage | 意味漏出 | A transition in meaning across a boundary whose crossing becomes invisible. | The transition is not necessarily invalid; the failure is that it becomes silent. | [Vol. 3 §15](./docs/en/vol3-basics-of-sim.md#15-semantic-leakage) |

## Meaning Handling Boundary

The following terms are intentionally distinct:

```text
Unassigned
    semantic handling has not yet been decided
    temporary observation state

Meaning Not Required
    semantic meaning is not required for the current context
    legitimate terminal handling

Undefined
    current semantic structure cannot explain where the element belongs
    normally directs inquiry toward further observation

The Right Not to Mean
    constraint on the observer
    meaning must not be forced merely because it can be constructed
```

## Translation Boundary: Recurrent and Reflexive

The terms **Recurrent Observation** and **Reflexive Observation** are intentionally distinct.

```text
Recurrent Observation / 再起観測
    observation feeds another observation
    temporal/process re-entry

Reflexive Observation / 再帰観測
    observation turns toward the observer or observation conditions
    observation itself becomes observable
```

The Japanese terms `再起` and `再帰` are therefore not spelling variants in this canon. They identify different concepts.

## Translation Boundary: The Right Not to Mean

The official Japanese translation of **The Right Not to Mean** is **意味を持たない自由**.

The word `right` is used here to protect the legitimacy of not assigning meaning; it is not intended primarily as a legal-right claim. The Japanese translation uses `自由` to emphasize freedom from compelled meaning attribution.

## Use of This Index

When terminology appears inconsistent, first determine whether the difference is:

1. an intentional semantic distinction,
2. an official translation choice,
3. a translation or terminology defect,
4. or an unresolved difference requiring observation.

Do not normalize terminology before that distinction has been observed.
